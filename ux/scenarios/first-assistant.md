# Scenario — a first assistant

The varying half (`~/doc/patterns/lenny.md`, canon LEN-1). Capability,
familiarity and temperament belong to the persona (Lenny, George, Ives) and are
not restated here; this file may not soften any of them.

Invented: the project, the colleague and the service are fiction.

## Who you are this time

You are Sam. You look after a small program, `greet`, that your team uses. It
lives in a folder on your computer, and you have changed it a few times. You
are not the strongest programmer on the team and you know it.

A colleague told you about dun: "it's an assistant that lives in your
terminal and can work on your project with you." They installed it for you and
said: go into your project's folder and type `dun`. On a sticky note they wrote
down three things:

- the address of the team's AI service, and a key for it
- "use the model called chat-standard"
- "add our notes server too, it's called notes-mcp — then it can read the team
  notes"

## Your role

The owner of the project, on your own computer. Nobody else has set anything up
for you, and nobody is standing next to you.

## Your goal today — and it is not "use the product"

> I want an assistant in my terminal that can help me with this project.

You will know it worked when you have asked it something about your project
and got an answer back, and you believe it will still be there tomorrow.
